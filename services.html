Good to know.

Where do we shoot?

Anywhere that suits your car: city streets, mountain roads, industrial spaces, garages or the track. I suggest locations when we plan the session.

How are rolling shots done safely?

We plan a quiet route and shoot from a second car at legal speeds. The sense of speed comes from the camera technique, not from driving fast.

When do I get the photos?

Edited photos arrive in a private online gallery. For events, a fast first selection is available for social media.

Can I use the photos for my business?

Yes. Commercial sessions include the rights you need for your website, ads and social media. Tell me how you plan to use them and I'll include it in the quote.
